# Driven side snow and disappearing vehicle snow — 2026-09-18

Snowfall deposits snow independently on the four car-local sides (+X, -X, +Z, -Z). The amount starts from zero on older saves. Actual snowfall below freezing and relative airflow are required. Wind coats parked cars too; a calm stationary car does not gain a new layer. Relative airflow is world wind minus car velocity, transformed to the car frame. Windward faces gain snow faster, while turbulence produces weaker deposits on other faces. The extra motion turbulence smoothly increases from 30 to 70 km/h. Changing wind or turning does not transfer existing snow between faces.

The logical controller uses a 0.25 s cadence, reusable collections and no renderer scans or new GPU surfaces. WALKABLE-compatible shelter probes are queued fairly, at most four per frame. Unknown or stale shelter suppresses deposition until checked. Existing snow remains during stops and dry, cold weather. Current locomotive heat reduces sticking and melts accumulated snow; positive air temperature also melts it. The existing roof heat history is intentionally not multiplied into side rendering a second time.

The existing procedural GBuffer pass uses two additional per-car vector arrays: accumulated amounts and local-to-world rotations. Rotating the native unit surface normal selects the face without differentiating quantized half-float positions; car-local grain retains its placement through rotation and floating-origin moves. Thin snow grows in opacity and patch coverage without a minimum logical coverage threshold. Cab/interior exclusion IDs, static scenery and downward-facing undersides receive no side snow. Existing roof coverage and the rolling-stock limit remain in force. No extra full-screen pass, per-car render texture or synchronous readback is introduced.

The subsequent in-game report exposed a simulation/render integration gap. The old quarter-second simulation rounded every amount below 0.00001 to zero: light snowfall at 31–35 km/h never accumulated. Three hard speed gates also blocked stationary crosswind, and the rank-based rendering threshold concealed small and heat-limited deposits. Positive increments are now retained, exposure checks admit wind-driven stationary deposition, and the shader shows thin dusting continuously. Snowfall intensity remains linear, with a 90-second unit-impact accumulation time. Existing 20-second performance reports now include tracked/depositing/sheltered car counts, maximum side amount and snowfall, collected without extra object scans.

Persistent values use stable car GUIDs. Snow save v2 stores four amounts and reads v1 saves. Network protocol 10 sends bounded snapshots at most once per second, with quantized amounts; weather-only updates omit the fleet array. Late joiners receive the latest host history. All multiplayer participants need this build. The mod version remains 0.3.3.

The supplied Player.log contains a concrete render failure after entering a green gondola: `Renderer.get_isPartOfStaticBatch` throws from `SnowVehicleRegistry.RefreshExclusionMeshUses`. A destroyed Unity renderer still passes the CLR `is MeshRenderer` check. The rebuild now checks Unity object lifetime before accessing native properties, including the mesh-use counting pass. The yard regression destroys a renderer between preparation and command recording and verifies that the fleet remains rendered.
