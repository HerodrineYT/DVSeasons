# Dynamic Seasons for Derail Valley

`DVSeasons` is a separate Unity Mod Manager package. It does not patch jobs, tracks, junctions, cars, or saves owned by other mods.

## Runtime design

- Surface snow uses authored winter PNG albedos only. `SeasonalTextureController` clones native material slots on allowed concrete, cobblestone and roof renderers, then binds the existing CPU texture transition queue. It neither installs a snow shader nor changes mesh geometry/UVs. Readable slots require at least 85% upward-facing sampled area (normal.y >= 0.38); mixed wall/roof slots are left unchanged. Unreadable geometry accepts dedicated roof tiles or flat bounds only. Cloned slots and original textures are restored on reset. Exact names are in `SnowSurfaceProfile`.
- The authoritative season phase is a number in `[0, 4)`: spring, summer, autumn, winter.
- Progress is driven by the game's `WeatherPresetManager.DateTime`, only after `WorldStreamingInit.LoadingFinished`. UMM's `OnSessionStart` binds the new save before weather restoration completes; `UnloadWatcher.UnloadRequested` suspends the cycle and clears scene-specific state. The clock is rebased on each world load and after losing the native time source, so menu/loading time and gaps between different saves never advance the calendar. The real-time fallback is restricted to an active, fully loaded game session.
- `SaveGameManager.OnInternalDataUpdate` stores a versioned `DVSeasons.*` payload containing double-precision phase, automatic-cycle mode, days per season, random/manual transition mode and the already selected transition season/duration. Loading a save restores this calendar before rendering and does not reroll the transition. Version-1 payloads migrate with random duration enabled, matching their original behavior. Saves without the payload migrate the legacy UMM checkpoint; a new game starts at `StartingSeason`. UMM also receives a fallback checkpoint on manual changes, every 30 seconds of gameplay and on exit. A latched multiplayer-client flag prevents host settings from overwriting that local checkpoint even after the networking singleton has shut down. Native save-data events are used without Harmony save patches; unrelated save keys are untouched.
- The seasonal AssetBundle is loaded lazily and revalidated using Unity object lifetime semantics. If the game unloads it on return to the menu, the next session reloads and reindexes it. Terrain/vegetation bindings, generated textures, shader overrides and scan timers are reset between worlds. Missing-bundle retries are throttled and do not emit a warning for every terrain binding on every frame.
- Transition duration has two UMM modes. Random mode is enabled by default and selects one retained integer from one to five game days for each new season. Disabling it activates a manual 1–5 day slider; that value remains unchanged across season boundaries. Both modes are clamped to the season length. Visual and physical profiles use smoothstep interpolation during those final days.
- Winter precipitation reads the authoritative host `WeatherDriver.RainValue` and drives an always-simulated world-space snow volume positioned ahead of the camera. Multiplayer protocol 6 sends transition-duration mode, rain intensity, horizontal wind velocity and sunlight factor once per second, so a client reacts to host weather-editor changes even though the base Multiplayer mod only supplies its native weather snapshot during world loading. A Harmony prefix clears and pauses every emitter owned by the native `DV.Rain.RainParticles` component while snow replaces rain, so no drop particles remain mixed into snowfall. There is no synthetic ambient snowfall during dry game weather. On the autumn-to-winter edge, rain-to-snow conversion is gated to begin about five in-game minutes before the first complete MicroSplat layer changes; after that point visibility follows the square-root snow curve. Particles randomly select from a project-owned 4×4 transparent atlas of sixteen irregular soft snow clumps, with randomized billboard rotation and size. Host wind controls horizontal drift, turbulence, and an upwind emitter offset. The particle material follows host sunlight, keeping night snow dim blue-gray instead of unlit white. Two forward roof probes clear the particles in tunnels, while a close roof probe moves the spawn volume beyond a cab windscreen. The optimized emitter caps itself at 7500 particles and does not run per-flake 3D world collision.
- Window materials and the native `DropletSimulationB` bindings are never patched by DVSeasons. Rain drops, condensation and wiper clearing therefore remain entirely vanilla even while world-space winter precipitation is rendered as snow. Surface-only rain artifacts are suppressed separately: `ParticleWetSplatter` cannot spawn collision decals, `DripLine` emitters are paused and `RainRipples` receives zero temporary rain/ripple/flow values while rendering snowy precipitation.
- Seasonal weather retains the native driver and its random noise, with smoothly blended rain thresholds and seasonal offsets to the fog/cloud noise samples before presets, rain and wind are derived. Explicit weather-coordinate/preset overrides bypass the noise modification. `SeasonalClimateController` also remaps the solar calculation to 12 + 4*sin(phase*pi/2) daylight hours while retaining native solar noon; it does not write Cycle.Hour, DateTime or DayLengthInMinutes. Sunrise/sunset and the drying sunlight factor follow the remap. Five reversible Harmony patches are scoped to the active world's driver/sky; the weather transpiler verifies the DV99 layout.
- While native rain is represented as winter snow, Harmony silences `EnvironmentRainManager`, the locomotive `RainAudioModule`, and the wet day/night variants maintained by `EnvironmentSoundSystem`. The suppression remains active throughout snowy seasonal states, avoiding one-frame leaks around vanilla rain fades; non-wet biome loops are not modified. Winter additionally owns a reversible zero override of `WeatherDriver.ThunderValue`, stopping thunder and lightning while wind, dry biome ambience, wetness and the native rain value continue normally.
- Terrain materials are tracked both through loaded `Terrain` components and through the exact built-in renderer MicroSplat signature used by streamed landscape tiles. The main DV99 MicroSplat shader was built without its optional snow feature, so accumulation replaces complete `Texture2DArray` slices instead of blending pixels. The main `_Diffuse`, `_ClusterDiffuse2`, `_ClusterDiffuse3`, `_DistanceResampleHackDiff`, and exact built-in distant `_Splats` arrays all participate at different viewing distances. DV's built-in arrays are 1024px while the stable seasonal arrays are 512px, but both expose the same 16 logical layers. A 512px `RenderTexture` array is therefore created for each distinct real source array. `Graphics.Blit(Texture, RenderTexture, sourceDepthSlice, destinationDepthSlice)` selects and scales the real clear slice entirely on the GPU; the winter slice is written through the same path, and mipmaps are generated after the changed slices have been applied. This preserves each map's material numbering and removes the incompatible spring fallback responsible for the neon-green industrial rectangle. The material-free near `Terrain` used by DV99 exposes the same sixteen logical slots as individual `TerrainLayer` objects; those layers are switched directly to the matching bundled `TerrainTexture1…16` assets in the same distributed order. Renderer-only streamed tiles are accepted only when their material/shader/array names identify the built-in 16-layer MicroSplat set, so unrelated custom-map impostors remain untouched. At zero cover the actual originals are restored. Coverage advances to 4 of 16 layers at 25%, 8 at 50%, 12 at 75%, and all 16 at full cover; thaw restores the real clear slices/layers in reverse order. No terrain pixel readback is performed. Original materials, layer textures and grass tints are restored when the mod stops.
- Derail Valley build 99 terrain layers, vegetation, track materials and 17 exact road/pavement/roof albedo names are matched against their original textures. The stable terrain/vegetation AssetBundle remains compatible with the previous release, while loose PNG overrides are preferred whenever present. `Textures/Seasonal/winter_track/{early,middle,late}` exposes 24 logical atlases for `RailMed_d`, `RailOld_d`, three full-resolution ballast types, `BallastLODMed`, and both sleeper types. Twenty distinct PNG files are packaged; four byte-identical sleeper slots resolve through loader aliases. The normal winter folder additionally contains `AsphaltRoad_01d`, `AsphaltTiling_01d`, `RoadDetail`, `Roads_LOD_01d`, `Sidewalk_01d`, `SidewalkTiles_01d` and the lightly snow-covered `MB_concrete_01d`; these blend directly against `SnowAmount` in the priority queue. The source Unity project contains 171 `Texture2D` inputs, including both ice textures and SnowSurfaceDense. Roof/pavement/road albedo now uses CPU-composited growing snow patches: 22%, 65%, 95% (98% roofs) area at SnowAmount 0.28, 0.62, 1.0; alpha and original native shaders are preserved. A pure core profile selects snow-free/early/middle/late track anchors at coverage 0, 0.28, 0.62 and 1.0 and applies smoothstep inside each interval. Consequently the autumn-to-winter path reaches maximum snow exactly at winter, while winter-to-spring traverses the same assets in reverse. Autumn sleepers use the supplied fallen-leaf atlas without runtime recoloring. Unknown custom-map textures remain unchanged instead of being procedurally recolored.
- Winter puddle ice subscribes to WetStuff.AfterDecalRender and changes only smoothness (GBuffer1 alpha) inside the wet mask. The linear temporary specular copy preserves native RGB reflectance. GBuffer0 is never copied or bound, so no winter colour grading/albedo tint is applied to wet vehicles or buildings. Dry pixels are discarded; ice texture modulates roughness. There are no world-space helper meshes. Wetness, native puddle spawning and world-water ice remain intact.
- `WaterLake` uses the exact WaterIceController and ice PNGs from 0.2.6: native BadDog/BGWater plus the stock transparent diffuse overlay at queue 3100 and 34% maximum opacity. Readable water submeshes receive a CPU copy with world-XZ UVs at 22-metre scale; unreadable meshes retain original UVs. Clones are disposed on reset. The later bundled WaterIceOverlay shader is no longer used by this controller. Puddle screen-mask fixes and texture-only snow remain unchanged.
- Transition work is explicitly frame-budgeted. Runtime texture interpolation processes at most 32,768 pixels per configured update slot and uploads only a completed texture. Terrain vegetation parameters use quantized visual steps. Per-instance tree recoloring is deliberately avoided because assigning a complete `TerrainData.treeInstances` array forces Unity to rebuild the entire tree population and caused the largest transition hitch; winter leaf density and distant brightness instead come from the fixed foliage and billboard profiles.
- Winter adhesion uses `WeatherDriver.WetnessValue`. Derail Valley build 99 maps wetness `0..0.5` to up to 50% native friction reduction, so the GUI deliberately clamps the equivalent wetness to `0.5`.

## Multiplayer

`DVSeasons.Multiplayer.dll` is loaded only when `MultiplayerAPI` is present. The host owns the phase and broadcasts a versioned state packet once per second and after manual changes. Protocol 6 also carries the authoritative random/manual mode, transition season and transition duration. A changed mode or value is force-broadcast immediately, and clients apply it without selecting their own duration. Clients request a state on connect; `OnPlayerReady` supplies late-join snapshots. Clients never advance or author the cycle.

## Compatibility

- **Passenger Jobs:** no Passenger Jobs objects are read or changed.
- **DoubleTrack:** no topology is cached. Active terrain materials and exact `DV/DistantTerrain` instances are rescanned, so streamed terrain loaded or changed after DoubleTrack remains eligible for seasonal visuals.
- **Custom cars and other mods:** snow and adhesion use game-global weather plus terrain shader capabilities. The mod does not rewrite car prefabs or cargo/job data.
- **External weather overrides:** `RespectExternalWetnessOverride` avoids taking ownership if another system already overrides wetness when DVSeasons first attaches.

## Known limits of the first playable build

- Snow coverage depends on whether a terrain material exposes one of the known built-in snow properties. Unsupported custom-map shaders still receive falling snow and foliage tint, but not guaranteed ground accumulation.
- Automatic vegetation discovery uses material, shader, and texture names. A custom shader or texture outside the DV99 fixed pack needs an explicit compatibility texture and rule.
- Texture arrays and procedural shaders without a `Texture2D` albedo source are deliberately skipped. Original material properties and `TerrainLayer.diffuseTexture` references are restored only when they are still owned by DVSeasons, so later changes made by another mod are not overwritten during shutdown.
- Streamed MicroSplat bindings are revalidated every frame. Stale terrain arrays are identified by their actual AssetBundle object identity (including the packed `terrain_winter` transition array), then repaired to the current cover stage. This includes a forced return to the original array at zero snow while unrelated arrays installed by other mods are left untouched.
- During winter-to-spring thaw, foliage and terrain-detail density remain in their winter state until snow drops below 6%. Their final return is blended across the last part of the thaw, so deciduous leaves and most grass do not reappear through an established snow cover.
- During autumn-to-winter accumulation, the vegetation curve is deliberately front-loaded: at 3% snow cover deciduous foliage is already about 37% of the way to its sparse winter state, so leaf loss leads visible ground accumulation instead of lagging behind it.
- Distant tree billboard profiles are deliberately dimmed in every non-summer season, with the strongest reduction in autumn and winter. Deciduous winter billboards combine relative-green detection with a compact-shape alpha mask, then classify remaining alpha components by geometry. Elongated branch/trunk strokes and substantial vertical components survive regardless of their position in the generated atlas; compact dark leaf islands are removed. Evergreen names are detected from both material and texture descriptions and bypass leaf removal when that metadata survives Unity's runtime billboard generation.
- Summer and winter retain the same captured `Terrain.treeDistance`, `TerrainData.treeInstances`, and therefore the same visible tree coordinates at maximum range. Winter extends the 3D-to-billboard switch through the middle distance so real leafless trunk-and-branch meshes remain visible around valleys; the far zone uses generated bare-tree billboards with a non-dilating coverage-preserving mip chain. No tree is invented, moved, or dropped. `detailObjectDistance` grows by only 20%, capped at 500 m, and winter detail density remains reduced to 8%. Changed distances blend with vegetation winter weight and return to their captured original values during thaw or mod shutdown.
- After component filtering, retained winter billboard strokes receive stronger alpha, a low-alpha one-pixel dilation, and a darker 0.46 brightness profile. This preserves thin sub-pixel branches under bilinear minification against bright snow without changing tree positions or requiring farther full-mesh rendering. The atlas is generated incrementally once per seasonal style step rather than processed every rendered frame.
- Distant ground support is intentionally restricted to the exact built-in `DV/DistantTerrain` shader and its 16-layer `_Splats` property. Custom-map imposters remain unchanged until they provide an explicit compatibility rule; this avoids corrupting unrelated terrain-like and billboard materials.
- Rain-to-snow particle detection is name-based and reversible. It does not alter the native rain value or wetness simulation; the dedicated rain loops are muted only while winter rain is rendered as snow.
- A full in-game smoke test is still required for each custom map and shader pack.
