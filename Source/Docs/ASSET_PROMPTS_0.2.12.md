Промпт:

Use case: photorealistic-natural
Asset type: seamless tileable game albedo texture, square 1024x1024.
Primary request: dense settled snow surface for winter rooftops and pavement. Entire image is flat matte snow, viewed orthographically straight down, fine natural snow grains and subtle irregular powder mottling at multiple scales.
Lighting: diffuse neutral flat albedo only, no baked lighting or directional shadows. Neutral pale gray-white, restrained contrast, no blue tint, no pure blown white.
Constraints: seamless matching opposite edges; uniformly detailed across frame; no footprints, ice cracks, ridges, dunes, objects, text, border, watermark or perspective. Not a landscape or a rendered scene. One snow material texture only.
